# Visual Digest App - General Info

## What is it?
A weekly digital magazine showing top app downloads in categories like Games, Productivity, Social, Health, and Education. AI agents create short summaries and visuals, compiled into a PDF/HTML magazine.

## How it works
- AI agents check app stores for popular apps.
- Team members vote on content using a simple blockchain system.
- A new magazine is created every Monday, available as a PDF or webpage.

## Who’s it for?
Anyone interested in app trends! Team members can join to vote or contribute ideas.

## Access
- Local: Check `./output` folder for magazine.
- Online: (Optional) Hosted on free platforms like Render.

Contact the team to join the DAO or suggest new app categories!
